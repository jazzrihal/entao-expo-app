# Então

Então is an iOS app for one signed-in person. This repo is the client. Auth and data are in Supabase.

You capture a photo, with an optional caption and location, and set it to Friends, Public, or Private. Home shows the feed, Friends groups posts by person, and Profile shows your own grid. You can like a post, edit or delete your own, and pin one for yourself. A new post can stay on the device and upload later. You can also save a moment: a time and a place.

You search for people and become friends, up to 150. You can report a post or profile, and block someone. With permission, a notification opens the related post, profile, or friends list.

iOS requirements: [docs/SRS.md](docs/SRS.md).
